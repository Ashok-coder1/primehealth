Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are always welcome. Booking online simply helps us prepare and reduces your waiting time.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Daily 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM. Doctor availability: OPD: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM.

Do I need to fast before a blood test?

For fasting blood sugar and lipid profile, please fast for 8–12 hours (water is fine). Most other tests don't need fasting – ask us if unsure.

How soon will I get my reports?

Most routine lab reports are ready the same day. Some special tests may take 1–3 days. X-ray and ECG reports are usually ready within a few hours.

How will I receive my report?

You can collect a printed copy at the clinic, or we can send a digital copy on WhatsApp or email.
